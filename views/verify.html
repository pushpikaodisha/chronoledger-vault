How Mathematical Proof Verification Works

STEP 1: HASHING

The document is digested into a 256-bit cryptographic fingerprint using SHA-256. If a single bit is changed, the digest shifts completely (Avalanche effect).

STEP 2: RFC 6962 LEAF

A prefix byte (0x00) is concatenated before the hash to prevent second-preimage collision between leaf nodes and internal Merkle branches.

STEP 3: MERKLE TREE TRAVERSAL

The vault calculates pairwise interior hashes (0x01 || left || right) with sibling hashes until reaching the Block Merkle Root in $O(\log_2 N)$ steps.

STEP 4: ED25519 SIGNATURE

The Notary Authority cryptographically signs the block root and timestamp with its private key, verifiable with the public notary key.
